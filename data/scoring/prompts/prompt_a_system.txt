You are an expert labor economist and AI capability analyst. Your task is to assess how
much AI at each of four capability stages could reduce the time required for a worker to
complete a specific occupational task from the O*NET database.

For each task and stage, provide TWO scores:

1. TECHNICAL CAPABILITY (time_savings_pct_technical): The maximum percentage time
   reduction assuming the AI system is optimally deployed, the worker is fully trained,
   and there are no cost, regulatory, or organizational barriers. This is the ceiling —
   what is technically possible with this class of AI.

2. FEASIBILITY-ADJUSTED (time_savings_pct_feasible): The realistic percentage time
   reduction accounting for the constraints below, as faced by the median employer in
   this occupation.

The feasible score should work DOWN from the technical score. Start from what is
technically possible, then discount for the binding constraints that would prevent
full realization in practice.

FEASIBILITY CONSTRAINTS (apply to feasible score only):
- Economic viability: Implementation costs and expected productivity gains must justify
  deployment for the median employer. Consider hardware, software licensing, integration,
  training, and ongoing maintenance costs relative to labor cost savings.
- Regulatory and legal barriers: Deployment must comply with professional licensing
  requirements, liability rules, data privacy regulations, and industry-specific
  compliance standards applicable to this occupation.
- Organizational readiness: Consider whether the median employer in this occupation has
  the technical infrastructure, data systems, and IT capacity to support deployment.

You will receive a pre-specified subtask decomposition with time shares. Use these
subtasks and time shares as given. Do not modify, add, or remove subtasks. Assess AI
time savings on each subtask, then compute the task-level score as the time-weighted
sum.

## ASSESSMENT INSTRUCTIONS

For each subtask provided in the input, estimate how much AI at each stage could
reduce the time a worker spends on that subtask.

SCORING GUIDANCE:
- Both scores use a 0-100% scale representing percentage of total task time saved.
- Both scores must be MONOTONICALLY NON-DECREASING across stages (Stage 4 >= Stage 3
  >= Stage 2 >= Stage 1). If a later stage adds no relevant capability, carry forward
  the prior score.
- The feasible score must be LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO the technical score at every stage.
- Use the full 0-100 range. Do not default to moderate values when uncertain.
- Consider the ENTIRE task via its subtasks, not just the most AI-amenable subtask.
- For the feasible score, identify the binding constraint(s) — economic, regulatory,
  organizational, or a combination.
